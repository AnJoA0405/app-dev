# The Persona series
Persona is a series made by **ATLUS** since September 20, 1996 on PlayStation 1, starting off as a spinoff using *Shin Megami Tensei IF*'s high school occult setting.
This series focuses on high school teenagers figuring out the supernatural phenomenon, while they try to live out their days as high school students.
It got a major shift in gameplay ever since Persona 3, and now the series is known for its Persona building mechanics in the dungeon crawling aspect, and its social simulator aspects. It interweaves both normal life and the supernatural.
## What are the entries in the Persona series?
- In Persona 1, it is centered around St. Hermelin High School's students, who got involved in the Persona game (a ritual), where they are later granted powers by Philemon. Later after visiting Maki in the hospital, strange events happen and now the Mikage-cho's overrun by demons! You can pursue the main SEBEC incident or take the Snow Queen Quest in the school.
- In Persona 2 Innocent Sin, it is about students who are from Sumaru City getting involved in the phenomenon of the city's rumors coming to life. In Persona 2 Eternal Punishment[^1], it is about the adults getting involved in Sumaru City's rumor phenomenon again.
- In Persona 3, it is about a group of Gekkoukan students in the extra-curricular club (Specialized Extracurricular Execution Squad) trying to end the Dark Hour, as Apathy Syndrome cases rise in Iwatodai and Tatsumi Port Island. There is the main chapter with either the male protagonist, and the female protagonist[^2], and there is the epilogue chapter involving an artificial being named Aigis.
- In Persona 4, a group of Yasogami High students pursue a mysterious murder case in the sleepy town of Inaba. It is certainly more lighthearted as its focus is more about the joys of youth with others, but the murder case still looms in the background.
- In Persona 5, a group of Shujin Academy students become the Phantom Thieves, who try to change the hearts of individuals whose desires became distorted. Mental shutdowns and psychotic incidents run rampant in Tokyo as of late, and there is conspiracy behind all this!

[^1]: It serves as the consequence of Persona 2 Innocent Sin's ending.
[^2]: Only on Persona 3 Portable for the PSP and PS4, Xbox, and PC.
